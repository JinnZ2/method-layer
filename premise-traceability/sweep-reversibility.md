## CHEMISTRY — INHERITS THE FALSIFIED PREMISE VERBATIM

  The principle of microscopic reversibility, as stated in chemistry,
  holds that microscopic dynamics is time-reversible BECAUSE the
  microscopic equations of motion are symmetric with respect to
  inversion in time.

  That is L1, imported WITH ITS JUSTIFICATION ATTACHED, in the version
  physics falsified in 2012. The import is not silent — the reason is
  carried across and restated. And the reason is now known false as
  stated.

  Second defect, same as L1's original grade: teaching sources state
  the principle is INFERRED FROM THE FACT that such processes CAN BE
  DESCRIBED by their equations of motion. Inferred from describability.
  A statement about the availability of a formalism, used as a
  statement about the world.

  Machinery on the chemistry side, present and NOT GRADED here:
  detailed balance, Onsager reciprocal relations, Wegscheider
  conditions, Tolman 1925.

## BIOLOGY — THE INSTRUMENTED ARM, AND WHAT IT SHOWS

  THE TEST IS REAL. Song & Magleby 1994 tested whether gating of a
  large-conductance Ca-activated K+ channel is consistent with
  microscopic reversibility, using two-dimensional dwell-time
  distributions. Their stated motivation is exactly the register's
  concern: IF THE ASSUMPTION IS INCORRECT, THEN THE MODELS AND
  ESTIMATED RATE CONSTANTS MADE WITH THE ASSUMPTION WOULD BE IN ERROR.
  A field instrumenting its own imported premise because the premise
  was load-bearing for its numbers. This is the behaviour the register
  says is missing upstream. It exists here.

  THE TEST HAS A KNOWN POWER FAILURE. Wagner & Timmer 2000: testing
  for detailed balance in aggregated Markov models suffers a LOSS OF
  POWER TO DETECT VIOLATIONS, caused by NON-IDENTIFIABILITY of the
  transition rates for models with EQUAL DWELL TIMES. For those model
  classes the instrument cannot resolve what it was built to detect.
  A 2002 follow-up is titled "Method-independent effect in testing for
  detailed balance in ion channel gating" — the blindness is not an
  artefact of one method.

  THE PREMISE IS THEN IMPOSED. A paper exists titled "HOW TO IMPOSE
  MICROSCOPIC REVERSIBILITY IN COMPLEX REACTION MECHANISMS."
  Reversibility here functions as a MODEL CONSTRAINT adopted to obtain
  identifiability, not as a finding.

  AND THE FIT IS DECIDABLE THE OTHER WAY. A PNAS result: if violations
  of detailed balance are REQUIRED to fit data, then NO MODEL OBEYING
  DETAILED BALANCE CAN FIT THE DATA AS WELL. So the constraint is not
  free — it is choosable, and choosing it costs fit.

# --- THE STRUCTURAL FINDING ---
# HELD FOR LATER. This is the item to raise.

  THE SECTOR WHERE A PREMISE IS MOST HEAVILY RELIED ON TO MAKE MODELS
  TRACTABLE IS THE SECTOR WHERE THE TEST PROVABLY CANNOT RESOLVE IT.

  TWO INDEPENDENT INSTANCES, SAME GEOMETRY:

    GRAVITY      Reversibility does its heaviest work here — the black
                 hole information paradox is a reversibility crisis —
                 and there is NO T MEASUREMENT in the sector at all.

    BIOPHYSICS   Reversibility is IMPOSED to obtain identifiability,
                 and the test is UNDERPOWERED exactly for the model
                 classes where identifiability fails. The imposition
                 and the blindness have the SAME CAUSE:
                 non-identifiability.

  PROPOSED MECHANISM (Claude's read, not established):
  this is a SELECTION EFFECT, not coincidence. A premise gets IMPOSED
  precisely where it cannot be measured, because that is where it is
  needed to close the model. Where it CAN be measured, it gets
  measured — and sometimes falsified, as in the weak sector — at which
  point it stops being a premise and becomes a result.

  CONSEQUENCE, AND THIS IS THE TRANSFERABLE PART:
  UNMEASURED STATUS IS NOT RANDOMLY DISTRIBUTED ACROSS A PREMISE'S
  SECTORS. IT IS CONCENTRATED WHERE THE PREMISE IS LOAD-BEARING.
  Therefore sampling a premise's grade in the sectors where
  measurement is EASY systematically OVERSTATES its support. The
  average grade across sectors is not an estimate of the grade where
  it matters. It is biased, and the direction of the bias is known.

  IF IT HOLDS, IT IS A CHECK, NOT A CRITIQUE:
  for any premise, do not ask "is it supported." Ask WHERE IT IS
  LOAD-BEARING, and grade THAT SECTOR SPECIFICALLY. Expect the
  load-bearing sector to be the least measured one. Expect the reason
  the measurement is missing to be the SAME reason the premise is
  needed.

  FALSIFIERS — NOT CHECKED:
  - A premise that is load-bearing in a sector where it is ALSO well
    measured. One clean case weakens the selection-effect account
    substantially. Not searched for.
  - A case where the imposition and the measurement failure have
    UNRELATED causes. In biophysics they share one cause
    (non-identifiability); if that is atypical, the geometry is
    coincidence.
  - Whether the pattern appears in fields with no formal models at
    all. Untested.
